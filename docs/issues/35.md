# grill-me word budget: rescind or re-derive the 500-word target

Source: issue 28 acceptance review (2026-11-25). Q9 of issue 28 said "keep the skill lean; target under 500 words (currently ~370)". The rewrite shipped at **712 words**, so the criterion fails as written — but whether the budget is the right measure is a deferred decision, not a blocker.

## Problem

Issue 28's 500-word target was set against the *pre-rewrite* skill (~370 words), when grilling was a leaner, prose-only recipe. The rewrite per Q4/Q13/Q15/Q16 deliberately adds, at issue-28's own direction:

- a glossary (7 defined terms),
- a full round template (intro, recap branches, frontier count, question blocks),
- four-stage question blocks (Abstract, Options, Recommendation, Reminder),
- a closing Legend template and facts-vs-decisions rules.

These grew the file by design. The acceptance criterion (<500 words) and the designed structure are now in direct conflict; something in issue 28's acceptance list is wrong, and it seems to be the number, not the structure.

## Desired behavior

Decide, for `skills/grill-me/SKILL.md`:

1. **Rescind the numeric budget** and replace it with a structural-likeness criterion ("no content beyond what the round/question/legend templates require"), or
2. **Re-derive a higher budget** from the shipped structure and record the reason, or
3. **Keep 500 and genuinely cut** — identify template redundancy (e.g. Abstract rules restating glossary terms) that can go without losing behavior.

The goal is lean-ness that survives Q6's positive-recipe rule: compression must come from removing duplication, not from stripping prompts to telegraphic bans.

## Settled decisions

| # | Topic | Decision |
